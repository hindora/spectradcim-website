Who this is for, and how it should feel

The decisions the tokens below follow.

  • Voice: a plain-spoken engineer. Calm, exact, no hype. Say what the product does and what it does not do yet.
  • Reader: the operations or facility lead first. It must still look credible when forwarded to the person who signs the budget.
  • Dark only. The product is a dark console; the site matches it. There is no light version.
  • One brand color family, from the logo. Blue fills actions, sky blue marks links and labels.
  • Red, amber and green mean status. They appear only for alarms and form feedback, never as decoration.
  • Flat. No gradients, no glow, no lift on hover. A border changing color is the hover signal.
  • Sentence case for every heading and label. Eyebrows and main buttons are set in capitals by the style sheet, so the source text stays in sentence case.
  • Real product screens carry the page. Simulated data is always labeled as simulated.

Layout and alignment

Eight rules. Each one exists because the mistake it prevents was found on a real page and fixed.

#RuleWhy
1A heading and its sentence stack vertically. Never side by side.Side by side, a large heading and small wrapped text never share a line.
2The eyebrow sits directly above its heading, on the same left edge. Never in a side column.In a narrow column, spaced capitals wrap and float away from the text they label.
3Captions and labels sit below a picture, aligned to its left edge. Nothing overlaps a screenshot.A label laid over a product screen hides part of the product.
4Eyebrows have no background box.A box turns a label into something that looks like a button.
5Heading block to the text that continues it: 24px inside a two-column section. To a full-width grid below it: 48px.A large gap after the intro makes the following paragraph look like a separate topic.
6Cards in one row share top and bottom edges, and their contents start from the top. A featured card differs by its border only.Cards of different heights put titles and links on different lines.
7Left-align by default. Center only a short section intro: eyebrow, heading, one sentence.Centered paragraphs and lists are hard to scan.
8Text lines run to 68 characters at most.Longer lines lose the reader on the way back to the left edge.
